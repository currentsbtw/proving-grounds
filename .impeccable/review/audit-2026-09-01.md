## Implementation Integrity Verdict

**CONDITIONAL PASS.** The implementation expresses a coherent, product-specific system on every surface the redesign actually reached, and nothing about it is interchangeable with a generic product. It fails the contract on two surfaces that the redesign did not finish converting — one of which is the only artifact that leaves the app.

**What the evidence supports (verified, not asserted):**

- The bundled detector returns `[]` over `src` and `public`.
- **The display face is confined to the wordmark in the app.** Computed font families across the live run: `IBM Plex Sans` on 365 elements, `Marcellus` on exactly **1** (`.pg-wordmark`). The remaining `Times New Roman` matches are `<head>`, `<script>`, `<meta>` and `<link>` — non-rendered nodes. `base.css:39` forces every `h1`–`h6` back onto the grotesk with a comment explaining why.
- **Ruled rows, not boxes, in the readout.** `.rd-block` carries `border-bottom` only; `.rd-seat` carries `border-top` only with the first row's rule suppressed; `.rd-chip`, `.pgp-btn` and `.rd-key` are all `border-radius: 0`.
- **Scryfall imagery is untouched.** No `filter`, `backdrop-filter` or `mix-blend-mode` anywhere in the app's CSS except one `brightness(1.08)` on a button hover. `CARD_ASPECT = 1.396` gives 0.7163 against Scryfall's own 0.71765 — `object-fit: cover` crops roughly 0.2% of the width, under a third of a pixel on a 150px card.
- **The Fan Content line ships in full in both places.** Byte-exact string match against the required sentence in `.pg-legal`; the same sentence, unabbreviated, in `shareImage.ts:563` and legible on the rendered PNG (633px wide at 9px inside a 1200px canvas, so it fits on one line without truncation).
- **No colour-only state anywhere checked.** Threat trend prints the word (`rising`/`steady`/`falling`); seat chips print `OUT`/`CLOCK`/`ARMED n+`/`HIT`; the meter is a `role="img"` with a full text label; the scorecard's event ledger prints the terminal word next to the colour.
- **The readout cannot disagree with the pod.** `seatToHit` in `SeatsBlock.tsx` calls the engine's own `livingSeats` / `highestThreatSeat`, the same functions the pressure system uses to pick targets.
- **CSS is not accumulating dead weight.** All 27 "orphaned class" candidates verified as false positives (dynamically composed `type-${}` / `kind-${}` / `evt-${}` / `is-${terminal}`, or a comment-only mention). Zero declared-but-unreferenced custom properties; every one of the four keyframes is referenced.

**What fails:**

- **The share image speaks a different language from the app it came from** (P1-3). It sets the deck name, the WIN/LOSS verdict chip and **every metric value** in Marcellus, and its body font is `system-ui` rather than IBM Plex Sans. OWN-WORLD says "the display face survives only in the wordmark" and "one grotesk family with true tabular numerals for every value." The app obeys both. The receipt does not.
- **The debrief is still the pre-redesign admin shell** (P1-4). `.sc-tile` is a bordered, rounded box on `--surface`; `.sc-section` likewise. The THESIS explicitly refuses "metrics as KPI tiles," OWN-WORLD asks for "ruled rows, not boxes," and FIRST VIEWPORT says the scorecard should be "restyled to the same slots." It has not been.
- Secondary drift: the accent appears at 54 sites and is worn by two chips in the readout rather than reserved for the one that needs action; a third, undeclared font stack is hardcoded in `decks.css`; `shareImage.ts` keeps a forked copy of the entire palette.

The play surface — the thing the contract was written for — passes. The verdict is conditional because the contract's own words go unmet on the exported artifact and the debrief.

## Executive Summary

- **Audit Health Score: 15/20 (Good — address weak dimensions)**
- **Issues found: 17** — P0: 0 · P1: 6 · P2: 6 · P3: 5
- Seven candidate findings were investigated and **dismissed as false positives** (listed at the end); none are reported above.

**Top 5:**

1. **One hardcoded hex breaks WCAG AA on the two primary actions in light mode.** `decks.css:52` sets `color: #17181c` — the dark theme's ground colour baked into `.dk-btn-primary`. When `--accent` darkens to `#7d5f16` for light, the text stays dark: **2.97:1** on "Start run" and on "Replay seed". Axe flagged both nodes with those exact colours.
2. **The run log cannot be scrolled from the keyboard.** `.hud-log-scroll` is `role="log"` with `scrollHeight > clientHeight` and `tabIndex: -1` (axe: `scrollable-region-focusable`, serious). PRODUCT.md makes keyboard-first a requirement and calls the log the product; a keyboard-only pilot can read only the visible slice.
3. **The share image violates the type contract** — Marcellus on the deck name, the verdict chip and every metric value; `system-ui` instead of the app's grotesk for body copy. This is the only artifact that leaves the app, and it is the one that does not look like the app.
4. **The scorecard still renders metrics as KPI tiles**, which the direction contract's THESIS names and refuses by name.
5. **22 controls fall below the 24×24 pointer-target minimum** (WCAG 2.5.8 AA) — the life steps at 25×20 and the `undo life` / `silhouette` / `add note` links at 16px tall, all of them in the readout the pilot works from every turn.

**Recommended next steps:** take the two contract failures first (share image typography, scorecard slots), then the four accessibility fixes, then the theming leaks. Full ordered list at the end.

### P1 — Major (fix before release)

#### [P1-1] Primary button ink is hardcoded, failing AA in light theme

- **Location**: `src/features/decks/decks.css:52` — `.dk-btn-primary { color: #17181c; }`
- **Category**: Theming / Accessibility
- **Evidence**: axe-core, light theme, two nodes: `.dk-deck-actions > .dk-btn-primary` ("Start run") and `.sc-actions > .dk-btn-primary` ("Replay seed") — *"insufficient color contrast of 2.97 (foreground #17181c, background #7d5f16)"*. The independent computed sweep returned the same 2.97:1. Dark theme passes.
- **Impact**: The single most important control on the pre-flight surface and the control that drives the product's sharpest feature (same-seed replay) are both below AA for any player on the light readout.
- **WCAG**: 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), AA.
- **Recommendation**: Replace the literal with a token that swaps — `--on-accent` (dark ink in dark theme, light ink in light theme) — or set the button's text to `var(--ground)`, which already inverts correctly across both palettes.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

#### [P1-2] The run log's scroll region is not keyboard-reachable

- **Location**: `src/features/hud/components/RunLog.tsx:114-115` — `<div className="hud-log-scroll" role="log" aria-live="polite">`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Evidence**: axe-core `scrollable-region-focusable` (serious) with the Log drawer open. Direct probe: `role: "log"`, `tabIndex: -1`, `scrollHeight > clientHeight`.
- **Impact**: PRODUCT.md Principle 3 is "the log is the product," and keyboard-first play is listed as a requirement rather than a nicety. A pilot who never touches the mouse can read only whatever slice happens to be in view; the rest of the run's record is unreachable.
- **WCAG**: 2.1.1 Keyboard, A.
- **Recommendation**: Give the scroll container `tabIndex={0}` and an accessible name (`aria-label="Run log"`). It already has `role="log"`, so it will announce correctly once it can be reached.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

#### [P1-3] The share image breaks the type contract

- **Location**: `src/features/scorecard/shareImage.ts` — `BODY_FONT` (line 59), `displayFont()` (line 149), and `display: true` at lines 400 (verdict chip), 410 (wordmark — permitted), 416 (deck name), 455 (every metric tile value)
- **Category**: Implementation Integrity
- **Evidence**: rendered PNG captured from the real "Share image" button (1200×600, 198 KB). The deck name, the LOSS chip and the values `1.3` / `never` / `0` / `43%` / `lost` are all set in Marcellus. `BODY_FONT` is `system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', …` — the app's `IBM Plex Sans` never appears.
- **Impact**: Two contract clauses broken at once — "the display face survives only in the wordmark" and "one grotesk family with true tabular numerals for every value." `system-ui` carries no guarantee of tabular numerals, so figures on the shared receipt can shift width, which is the exact failure `base.css:18` was written to prevent inside the app. The scorecard PNG is the artefact posted to Discord and Reddit; it is the app's public face and it does not match the app.
- **Recommendation**: Point `BODY_FONT` at the same stack as `--font-body`, and restrict `display: true` to the `PROVING GROUNDS` wordmark alone. Set the verdict chip, deck name and tile values in the grotesk with tabular numerals, matching `.sc-tile-value` — which already carries a comment stating this rule.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

#### [P1-4] The scorecard renders metrics as KPI tiles the THESIS refuses

- **Location**: `src/features/scorecard/scorecard.css:391` (`.sc-tile`) and `:428` (`.sc-section`); `src/features/scorecard/ScorecardPanel.tsx`
- **Category**: Implementation Integrity
- **Evidence**: `.sc-tile { background: var(--surface); border: 1px solid var(--line); border-radius: var(--radius); }` — a four-sided rounded box. `audit-scorecard-1440.png` shows seven such boxes plus two boxed sections. The readout next door is `border-bottom` only, square-cornered.
- **Impact**: The direction contract's THESIS refuses "the three-column admin shell with seats as sidebar cards and metrics as KPI tiles" by name; OWN-WORLD asks for "ruled rows, not boxes"; FIRST VIEWPORT says the between-runs surfaces should be "restyled to the same slots." The debrief is where the STORY says the pilot reads "the same slots filled in over time," and it is the one surface that still looks like the thing the redesign was built to replace.
- **Recommendation**: Restyle `.sc-tile` and `.sc-section` to the readout's grammar — small-caps printed label over a large tabular figure, separated by a single rule, no surface fill, no radius. `.sc-tile-value` already uses the right font and numerals, so this is a container change.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

#### [P1-5] Zone stacks are `role="button"` wrapping focusable children

- **Location**: `src/features/table/ZoneStack.tsx` → `.tbl-stack.is-wide.tbl-drop[role="button"][tabindex="0"]`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Evidence**: axe-core `nested-interactive` (serious), present in every scan of the live surface — *"Element has focusable descendants."* Node: `aria-label="Command zone: 1 cards"`.
- **Impact**: Assistive technology cannot resolve what a single activation is meant to do when a control contains other controls; the announced name of the outer button also masks the inner ones. It also puts an extra tab stop in front of the card the pilot actually wants.
- **WCAG**: 4.1.2 Name, Role, Value, A.
- **Recommendation**: Either drop `role="button"`/`tabindex` from the container and give the browse action its own real `<button>` in the stack header, or make the card children non-focusable while the stack owns the interaction. The first is closer to how the header already works.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

#### [P1-6] 22 pointer targets fall below the 24×24 minimum

- **Location**: `src/features/readout/SeatsBlock.tsx` (`LifeSteps` buttons, `.rd-disclose`), `ReadoutColumn.tsx` / `SeatsBlock.tsx` (`.rd-quiet-btn`), `src/features/pressure/EventDock.tsx` (`.pgp-note-toggle`)
- **Category**: Accessibility / Responsive
- **Evidence**: measured bounding boxes in the live run at 1440×900 — life steps **25×20** (×16, four per seat plus four for the player), `.rd-disclose` "silhouette" **58×16** (×3), `.rd-quiet-btn` "undo life" **51×16**, `.pgp-note-toggle` "add note" **51×16**. All 22 are inside the readout the pilot uses every turn.
- **Impact**: A 20px-tall target that adjusts life by −5 sits 4px from one that adjusts by −1; a mis-hit is a silent, wrong mutation to the run's log, and the log is the scoring input. The height is the failing dimension in every case, so the fix is cheap.
- **WCAG**: 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum), AA (2.2).
- **Recommendation**: Raise the minimum box to 24px on the height axis for these controls — padding or `min-height`, not font size, so the readout's density and the row rhythm survive. The life steps in particular can take the extra 4px without changing the row count.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

### P2 — Minor

#### [P2-1] Dimmed 10px figures sit at 4.25:1

- **Location**: `src/features/hud/hud.css:127-135` (`.hud-log-turn`, `opacity: 0.75` over `--muted` at 10px); `.sc-hist-when` in `scorecard.css`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Evidence**: axe-core, dark theme: `.sc-hist-when` — *"4.25 (#888b92 on #262932)"*. Light theme: *"3.42 (#888780 on #fbf9f4)"*. Direct probe of `.hud-log-turn`: `--muted` at `opacity: 0.75` computes to **4.50:1** over `--ground` and **4.25:1** over the raised row background it actually sits on.
- **Impact**: The turn number is the log's index — the column a pilot scans to find the wipe. It is the smallest and dimmest text in the app and it is the text most likely to be looked for rather than read past.
- **WCAG**: 1.4.3 Contrast (Minimum), AA.
- **Recommendation**: Drop the `opacity: 0.75` and let `--muted` carry the whole difference; the token already clears AA on both row backgrounds at full strength. Same for `.sc-hist-when`.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

#### [P2-2] Scrims and shadows do not participate in the theme swap

- **Location**: `hotkeys.css:7` `rgba(10, 11, 13, 0.72)`, `hotkeys.css:19` `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`, `readout.css:641` `rgb(0 0 0 / 28%)`, `table.css:790` `rgba(10, 11, 13, 0.72)`, `table.css:369/472/697` and `decks.css:257`
- **Category**: Theming
- **Evidence**: measured with the light theme active and the Keys overlay open (`audit-light-keys-1440.png`): `--ground` is `#f5f2ec` while the overlay's background computes to `rgba(10, 11, 13, 0.72)` and the panel's shadow to `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`. The drawer's shadow stays `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.28)`.
- **Impact**: OWN-WORLD says the other theme is "a token swap, never a redesign." A near-opaque near-black scrim over a warm paper ground is the one place where the light readout stops being the same design and becomes a dark design with light patches.
- **Recommendation**: Add `--scrim` and a shadow colour to `tokens.css` and give them light-theme values (a warm dark at lower alpha, and a softer shadow); replace the eight literals with those tokens.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

#### [P2-3] `shareImage.ts` keeps a forked copy of the whole palette

- **Location**: `src/features/scorecard/shareImage.ts:32-47` — `const C = { ground: '#17181c', surface: '#1f2127', raised: '#262932', line: '#33353c', ink: '#e8e6e1', muted: '#a0a3aa', accent: '#c9a85c', danger: '#e58a76', ok: '#8fc49e', manaW…manaG }`
- **Category**: Theming / Implementation Integrity
- **Evidence**: all thirteen values are byte-identical to the dark block in `tokens.css` today, with no link between the two files.
- **Impact**: A canvas cannot read CSS custom properties, so a literal copy is a defensible technique — but nothing keeps the copy honest. The next colour change lands in `tokens.css` and the share image silently keeps the old one, and the divergence only shows up on somebody else's Discord.
- **Recommendation**: Read the values at render time with `getComputedStyle(document.documentElement).getPropertyValue('--ink')` and friends, falling back to the current literals when the document is absent (the headless `verify-share-image.ts` harness). One function, and the fork stops being able to drift.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

#### [P2-4] The active event is not announced, while lesser tells are

- **Location**: `src/features/pressure/EventDock.tsx:501-503` — `<section className="pgp-dock …" aria-label={…}>`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Evidence**: live-region inventory of the running app returns four regions — `@dnd-kit`'s injected announcer, `.rd-dead-slot`, and both `.rd-tell-slot`s. `.pgp-dock` is a labelled `<section>` with no `role="status"` and no `aria-live`.
- **Impact**: The comment in `ReadoutColumn.tsx` says the tell slots "stay mounted and usually empty, so a tell is announced when it arrives." The active event — the thing that demands an answer on `1` or `2` and the only thing in the app with a deadline — gets no such treatment. A screen-reader pilot learns about the event only by going to look for it.
- **WCAG**: 4.1.3 Status Messages, AA.
- **Recommendation**: Put a mounted `role="status"` wrapper around the dock's seat/class/prompt line, exactly as the tell slots do, so a new event announces itself without stealing focus.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

#### [P2-5] The page has no level-one heading

- **Location**: `src/App.tsx:104` — `<span className="pg-wordmark">Proving Grounds</span>`
- **Category**: Accessibility
- **Evidence**: axe-core `page-has-heading-one` (moderate) in every scan. Heading inventory in the live run: `H2 SEATS`, `H2 YOU`, `H2 TELLS` — the document starts at h2.
- **Impact**: Heading navigation has no root, and the app's own name is not part of the document outline.
- **Recommendation**: Make the wordmark an `<h1>`. `base.css:39` already forces every heading onto the grotesk, so add a `.pg-wordmark` rule restoring `var(--font-display)` — the wordmark is the one place the contract permits it.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

#### [P2-6] Input borders sit at 1.45:1

- **Location**: `src/styles/base.css:83` — `input, select, textarea { border: 1px solid var(--line); }`
- **Category**: Accessibility / Theming
- **Evidence**: computed non-text sweep — `input` border `rgb(51, 53, 60)` on `rgb(23, 24, 28)` = **1.45:1** dark, `rgb(208, 201, 185)` on `rgb(245, 242, 236)` = **1.48:1** light. For comparison, buttons use `--rule` and clear the bar at 3.44:1 / 3.29:1.
- **Impact**: The boundary of the seed field and the combat "take" input — the one number a pilot types under a deadline — is close to invisible. Buttons in the same rows already do this correctly, so the inconsistency is the tell.
- **WCAG**: 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast, AA.
- **Recommendation**: Switch the input border from `--line` to `--rule`, matching the button treatment that already passes.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

### P3 — Polish

#### [P3-1] The accent is spread across 54 sites

- **Location**: 54 `var(--accent)` references across eight stylesheets — `table.css` 17, `pressure.css` 12, `scorecard.css` 9, `decks.css` 5, `hud.css` 4, `base.css` 3, `hotkeys.css` 2, `readout.css` 2
- **Category**: Implementation Integrity
- **Evidence**: `readout.css:164-168` gives both `.rd-chip.is-clock` and `.rd-chip.is-hit` the accent. Elsewhere it also carries focus rings, hover borders, drop targets, the commander card's border, `.tbl-counter.is-loyalty`, `.sc-term.is-unresolved` and a chart bar fill.
- **Impact**: OWN-WORLD reserves "one accent … for what needs action now (the active event, the seat to hit)." Most of the 54 are legitimate interaction affordances, but the readout itself spends the accent twice on a single seat row, and the scorecard spends it on a chart series. The `HIT` chip is the answer to "who do I hit" and it should be the loudest thing on the row.
- **Recommendation**: Keep the accent on `HIT` and the active event; move `CLOCK` to `--rule` (it already prints its own word, so nothing is lost) and take the accent off the chart-bar fill.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`

#### [P3-2] Two token values are hand-copied into literals

- **Location**: `src/features/table/table.css:639` `rgba(143, 196, 158, 0.5)` (= `--ok`) and `:644` `rgba(201, 168, 92, 0.5)` (= `--accent`)
- **Category**: Theming
- **Impact**: Both are borders on `.tbl-counter` variants. They will not change with the theme and will not follow a future palette edit — the rest of the file already uses `color-mix(in srgb, var(--accent) …%, transparent)` for exactly this, four lines away.
- **Recommendation**: Replace both with the `color-mix` form already in use elsewhere in the same file.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

#### [P3-3] A third font stack is hardcoded outside the token system

- **Location**: `src/features/decks/decks.css:44` — `.dk-textarea { font-family: ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, 'Cascadia Mono', Consolas, monospace; }`
- **Category**: Implementation Integrity
- **Impact**: The contract names one grotesk plus a display face for the wordmark. A monospace for the decklist paste box is a reasonable product decision — a decklist is columnar text — but it is undeclared, so it is invisible to anyone reading `tokens.css` to learn the type system.
- **Recommendation**: Either declare it as `--font-mono` in `tokens.css`, or set the textarea in the grotesk with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`, which the app already applies globally.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

#### [P3-4] Table drop-target transitions are not covered by reduced motion

- **Location**: `src/features/table/table.css` — `.tbl-card.is-interactive`, `.tbl-stack`, `.tbl-lib-strip`, `.tbl-battlefield.tbl-drop`, `.tbl-hand.tbl-drop`
- **Category**: Accessibility / Performance
- **Evidence**: with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` emulated, all four `@keyframes` animations correctly go to `none`, but eight elements still report `transition-duration: 0.12s`. Only `.tbl-hand-slot .tbl-slot` is covered (`table.css:377`).
- **Impact**: Small — 110–120ms colour and border transitions on drop targets. Noted because the rest of the codebase handles reduced motion so deliberately that this reads as an oversight rather than a decision.
- **Recommendation**: Extend the existing `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` block in `table.css` to the remaining drop-target selectors.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable animate`

#### [P3-5] A dead band sits between the YOU block and the pinned foot

- **Location**: `src/features/readout/readout.css` — `.rd-scroll` / `.rd-pinned`
- **Category**: Responsive / Layout
- **Evidence**: measured at 1440×900 — the `YOU` block's turn buttons end around y=585 and `.rd-pinned` begins at y=688: roughly **100–140px of empty column**, and the same gap is visible in `audit-1024.png`. `.rd-scroll` never overflows at any width or height tested, so the space is unused rather than reserved.
- **Impact**: Cosmetic. The contract asks for slots "ranked top to bottom by how often the pilot looks," and the gap pushes the active event further from the numbers above it than it needs to be. It also means the silhouette disclosures could stay expanded without costing anything.
- **Recommendation**: Let the scroll region grow into the space, or hold the pinned foot to the bottom and let TELLS occupy the slack when it has content.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`

## Patterns & Systemic Issues

**1. The redesign converted the play surface and stopped there.** The readout, table and shell obey the contract precisely — ruled rows, square corners, one grotesk, printed labels over tabular figures. The scorecard (P1-4) and the share image (P1-3) are the two surfaces that were not converted, and both fail contract clauses that the converted surfaces satisfy. This is a single unfinished job, not two unrelated defects, and the FIRST VIEWPORT clause already names it: "scorecard as today but restyled to the same slots."

**2. Every accessibility failure is off the primary play surface.** The live run scanned clean on contrast in both themes; all six AA violations sit in the deck rail, the log drawer, the scorecard, or on control sizing. The play loop got the attention; the surrounding surfaces inherited the pre-redesign state.

**3.
